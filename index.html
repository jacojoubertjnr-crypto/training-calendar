Staff Training Bookings

A WhatsApp service that helps school staff find and book professional development sessions.

How it works

  1. Weekly schedule. Staff who have signed up receive a WhatsApp message when the week's training schedule is published.
  2. Booking. They tick the sessions they want in a short form inside WhatsApp, and can change their choices at any time.
  3. Calendar. Each booking comes with an "Add to calendar" link.
  4. Reminder. On the morning of a session, they receive a reminder with an option to cancel.

Who receives messages

Only staff members who have asked to join, or whom the administrator added with their agreement. Anyone can stop receiving messages by asking the administrator to remove them.

Your information

The service stores each participant's name, WhatsApp number and session bookings, only to send schedules and reminders. It is not shared with third parties or used for marketing.
